📖 Kingdom Story

The Math Kingdom is under attack by powerful dark bosses, preventing students from restoring peace across the four learning zones.

📜 RULES OF GAME

🎲 Gameplay Rules

1. The teacher registers 2–4 players. Players take turns in the order shown in the player list.

2. On their turn, the player rolls the dice and moves their token on the physical board. The teacher selects the zone and tile type where the player lands.

3. The player answers the mathematics question within the time limit.

4. A correct answer earns the points shown for that tile and any applicable reward.

5. An incorrect answer means the player stays on the current tile and receives no points for that question. On a Trap tile, the existing 10-point penalty also applies unless a Skip Trap Card is used.

6. There are no lives. Players keep their earned points and Math Crystals, subject to the stated tile and card effects.

7. Defeating a Boss awards points and a Math Crystal. The teacher manages progression between zones on the physical board.

8. After every player has completed the board and the required final challenge, the teacher selects VIEW RESULTS. The player with the highest total points is the Kingdom Champion. If there is a tie, the tied players share the top ranking.

🎯 Tile Types & Scoring System

🌳 Normal Tile

Correct answer rewards +10 points.

🎁 Bonus Tile

Correct answer rewards +20 points.

💣 Trap Tile

Correct answer rewards +5 points. Wrong answer gives −10 points.

🤝 Teamwork Tile

Correct answer rewards +15 points to all players.

🐉 Boss Challenge

Reach the end of each learning zone and complete the Boss Challenge to unlock the next learning zone. Boss: +30 points + Math Crystal

💎💎 Math Crystal Rewards & Learning Support

Math Crystals can be exchanged for Power-Up Cards to provide temporary learning support during gameplay.
